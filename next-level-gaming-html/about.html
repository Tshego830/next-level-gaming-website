THE NEXT LEVEL MINDSET

Big ambitions.
No barriers.

Next Level Gaming was founded by Ellen Phatedi, a lifelong gamer who grew frustrated by the lack of access to high-performance gaming hardware in South Africa.

Starting from a single location in Johannesburg, our mission is to make world-class gaming technology accessible to everyone — from casual players and competitive esports athletes to corporate teams looking for unique team-building experiences.

Today, the arena houses over 100 gaming rigs, dedicated VR suites, racing simulators, an escape room and a full tournament floor. Every piece of equipment is maintained to the highest standard and upgraded annually.

Walk in, sit down, and play at the highest level possible.
